# My Favorite Movie: As Above, So Below

## Movie Information
- **Title:** As Above, So Below
- **Genre:** Horror, Mystery, Thriller
- **Release Year:** 2014
- **Director:** John Erick Dowdle

## About the Movie
As Above, So Below is one of my favorite horror movies. The story follows a group of explorers who enter the catacombs beneath Paris to search for a mysterious stone. As they go deeper, they experience strange and scary events.

## Why I Like This Movie
I like this movie because it has a mysterious story and unexpected scenes. The movie makes me curious about what will happen next. I also enjoy how the characters face their fears while trying to escape.

## My Favorite Parts
1. When the group enters the catacombs.
2. When they discover strange things underground.
3. When they try to find their way out.

## My Rating
**9/10** - I recommend this movie to people who enjoy horror, mystery, and adventure.

## My Favorite Quote
> The only way out is down.

## Conclusion
For me, As Above, So Below is an interesting horror movie because of its story, mystery, and suspense. I have watched it many times, and I still enjoy it.
